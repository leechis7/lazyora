LazyOra v0.19.0 brings Results, Messages, and DBMS_OUTPUT into one result area.

- Press **Shift+Tab** in the result area to focus the tab strip, **Left/Right** to choose a tab, and **Enter** to open it. Mouse and **View** menu navigation are also available. **Alt+P** opens DBMS_OUTPUT directly.
- F9 and F5 keep the selected result tab and SQL editor focus. New content is marked with `*`; execution or output errors are marked with `!`.
- Results and messages belong to each SQL tab. DBMS_OUTPUT is shared within its connection. In the output tab, **Alt+E** toggles collection, **Ctrl+C** copies, and **Alt+D** clears the display.

An Oracle Free terminal test verified tab switching, focus, and DBMS_OUTPUT controls. The full terminal scenario did not finish because of a schema navigation timeout waiting for `CHANGED_AT`, also reproduced on v0.18.0.

AMD64 and ARM64 executables are available for Linux, Windows, and macOS. Verify them against `SHA256SUMS`.
