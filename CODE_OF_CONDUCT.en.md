# Code of conduct

[한국어 (primary)](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | **English**

Treat one another with respect in LazyOra issues, pull requests, reviews and project discussions. People should be able to participate regardless of experience, nationality, language, gender, disability or other personal background.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss problems, code and evidence without personal attacks.
- Accept differences of opinion and offer specific, actionable feedback.
- Protect personal information and private material.

Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, threats, sexual insults, unauthorized disclosure of personal information and repeated spam are not allowed.

## Reporting and action

Report concerns and relevant links to **leechis7@gmail.com**. Do not add personal information in public discussions.

Depending on the situation, the maintainer may request clarification or changes, remove inappropriate posts, lock discussions or restrict participation. Reports are handled within the scope needed for review.
